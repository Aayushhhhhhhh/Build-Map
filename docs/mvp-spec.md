# BuildMap Pune — MVP

## Product promise
Discover where the city is being built, then discover what you can buy there.

## First experience
1. Open Pune map
2. Search locality or project
3. Filter residential/commercial/mixed-use
4. Filter upcoming/under construction/ready/completed
5. Click a project
6. See progress, developer, RERA/source status and available inventory

## Design direction
Minimal, editorial, premium. Large map, restrained typography, white surfaces, subtle borders, compact controls and no visual clutter.

## Trust model
Every important project field will eventually show its source: RERA verified, developer provided, or community reported.

## V1 exclusions
Payments, loans, rentals, broker marketplace, nationwide coverage and investment predictions.
